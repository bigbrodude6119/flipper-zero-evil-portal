Immediate Assistance Resources

Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration (SAMHSA)

Emergency Note: If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, please call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room right away.

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Free, confidential support for anyone experiencing mental health-related distress, thoughts of suicide, or substance use crisis.

Call or Text: 988
Web: 988lifeline.org

SAMHSA’s National Helpline

Free, confidential, 24/7 treatment referral and information service for individuals and families facing mental and/or substance use disorders.

Call: 1-800-662-HELP (4357)
TTY: 1-800-487-4889

Homelessness & Housing Support

Find local emergency shelters, housing assistance programs, and community services in your area.

Web: FindTreatment.gov
HUD Support: HUD Find Shelter

National Domestic Violence Hotline

Highly trained expert advocates are available 24/7 to talk confidentially with anyone experiencing domestic violence or seeking resources.

Call: 1-800-799-7233
Text: "START" to 88788

Veterans Crisis Line

Connects Veterans in crisis and their families and friends with qualified, caring Department of Veterans Affairs responders.

Call: 988, then Press 1
Text: 838255

Disaster Distress Helpline

24/7 immediate crisis counseling for people experiencing emotional distress related to any natural or human-caused disaster.

Call or Text: 1-800-985-5990
